# Code of Conduct

OpenHome3D is a practical, welcoming space for people with different levels of coding, 3D, design, and GitHub experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Credit contributors and respect their authorship.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, or publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical.

This policy applies in the repository, Discussions, Issues, Pull Requests, and other spaces where someone represents OpenHome3D.
